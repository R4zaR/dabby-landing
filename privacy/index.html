Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 6, 2026

Thank you for using Dabby. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the Dabby app, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information. By using Dabby, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Dabby is a timer application that stores certain preferences and history data locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers. It includes:

  • Session history (preset used, heat and cool times, date)
  • Custom preset configurations (names, times, temperatures)
  • App preferences (selected theme, sound settings)

Dabby also uses third-party services that may collect certain information automatically, including:

  • Device identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID)
  • Device type, operating system version, and language settings
  • App usage data and interaction events
  • IP address (used to estimate general location for ad targeting)
  • Diagnostic and crash data
  • Purchase and subscription status

2. How We Use Your Information

Local device data (session history, presets, preferences) is used solely to provide app functionality and is never shared with us or any third party.

The information collected through third-party services is used to:

  • Display relevant advertisements within the app (free users only, via Google AdMob)
  • Measure ad performance and effectiveness
  • Process and manage in-app subscriptions and purchases (via RevenueCat and Google Play)
  • Improve app stability and diagnose technical issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to any third parties beyond what is described in this policy.

3. Third-Party Services

Dabby uses the following third-party services:

Google AdMob
Dabby displays advertisements for free users provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data about your device and app usage to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. Dabby Pro subscribers do not see ads. Google's data practices are governed by their own privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

RevenueCat
Dabby uses RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions and one-time purchases. RevenueCat may collect your device identifier and purchase history to verify and manage your subscription status. RevenueCat's privacy policy can be reviewed at:
https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Google Play Billing
All payments for Dabby Pro are processed securely by Google Play. Dabby does not collect or store any payment information. Google Play's privacy policy can be reviewed at:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

4. Data Retention

Data stored locally on your device (session history, custom presets, preferences) remains on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data. Dabby does not store this data on its own servers.

Any data collected by third-party services such as Google AdMob and RevenueCat is retained according to their respective privacy policies and data retention schedules.

5. Children's Privacy

Dabby is not intended for use by children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take steps to remove that information.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date at the top. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
